JOHAP original guide dataset, revision 2026-10-23.
The current overwatch.json and guide.json contain independently authored JOHAP
editorial analysis and factual hero/map identifiers from official Blizzard sources.
They replace the former third-party crawl at the same URLs. No NamuWiki prose,
ratings, summaries, footnotes or source-body hashes are used to generate this dataset.
Official mechanics and third-party video references are citations, not an endorsement
or a grant of rights in Blizzard artwork. See /guide-methodology.html and /credits.html.
The original editorial text may be used commercially by the JOHAP project owner.
